Syncer

An Obsidian community plugin that syncs references and links from your existing tools into a target Markdown note under a heading you choose.

Syncer runs on your computer inside Obsidian. It does not operate a Syncer backend, proxy, or hosted API. Tokens stay in the plugin’s local settings. Synced lines are written into your vault.

What it syncs

  • Google Tasks (incomplete tasks from lists you select)
  • Gmail Starred (subject and from of starred messages)
  • Microsoft To Do and Outlook flagged mail
  • Azure DevOps work items assigned to you
  • Firefox Bookmarks from a local profile (desktop)
  • Todoist tasks from projects you select

Install it from Obsidian’s Community plugins browser or the Community directory.
